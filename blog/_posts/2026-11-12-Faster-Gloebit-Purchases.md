We've been making Gloebit faster, and we want to share what's improved and what's coming next.

![Gloebit FinTech blog]({{ myPrimaryImageURL }} "Faster Gloebit purchases and approvals")

<!--end_excerpt-->

## Buying gloebits is much faster ##

[Buying gloebits](https://www.gloebit.com/purchase/) could take up to a minute. It is now much faster, and we are working to make it faster still.

Nothing changes in how you buy. Your gloebits simply arrive sooner.

## In-world payments ##

The same improvements should already make in-world payments between residents faster. We have more to do there, and additional improvements are coming soon.

## Faster merchant approvals and seller reviews ##

- **Merchant approvals:** most [requests to create an app](https://www.gloebit.com/merchant-signup/) and connect a grid to Gloebit are now approved quickly, so you can start setting up right away.
- **Seller reviews:** we now review [requests to sell gloebits](https://www.gloebit.com/seller-signup/) much faster.

## What we're working on next ##

We've heard from merchants and grid owners, and we're working on two things you've asked for:

- **Raising seller limits faster.** We're speeding up limit increases, and we'll raise limits ourselves as active sellers approach them.
- **Sell orders that close faster.** We're working on getting your gloebits sold sooner after you place a sale.

We'll share updates here as these land.

Questions or feedback? Email us at info@gloebit.com.
